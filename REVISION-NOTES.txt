PokeSurvivor revised build 2

Run Start Game.cmd after extracting the entire folder.

Enemy population:
- Total budget: min(40, 16 + floor((player level - 1) / 3)).
- Normal enemies leave two reserved slots for bosses. Boss arrivals can replace a normal enemy at the total cap.
- Standard enemies spawn at visible screen edges, inset by 24 world units. Positions are checked for traversable ground.
- Normal enemies more than 96 world units outside the viewport are removed without KO, XP or loot. Scripted bosses are retained.
- Removed the pressure system's extra 24-enemy overflow and delayed distant-enemy recycling.
- The host controls these rules in multiplayer; clients follow the host's enemy state.

Level progression:
- Evolution requirements come from the game's own evolution data. Gabite unlocks at 24; Garchomp at 48; Pidgeotto at 18.
- Basic low-stat Pokemon, including Pidgey, populate the early pool. Strong unevolved species have a stat-based minimum level.
- Newly unlocked species carry more encounter weight; older rookie species remain possible at reduced relative odds.
- Shared admission checks cover base-game spawns, waves and rare encounters. Wild evolution also checks the player's level.
- Scripted Legendary bosses retain their encounter identities. Ordinary evolved bosses obey the evolution gates.
- Normal HP, damage and speed scale by player level rather than elapsed run time. Special AI patterns begin at level 12.

Lake Trio dialogue:
- Replaced lonely/overdramatic solo lines with self-contained lines for Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf.
- Added two pair-specific chat lines per sister pairing and speaker.
- Named references to absent sisters are blocked. Plural sister/three-member lines require all three guardians alive and present.
- Spawning sisters do not count as present. A queued line is rechecked when it reaches the display.
